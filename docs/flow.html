4. The map

Hand-written, from OverlayWindowManager.show: read everything once (reload: list-windows with layout rects, list-workspaces, the focus), decide the summon (section 1), measure every window's size, follow AeroSpace's events, build the window and reveal it after 120 ms, then take the pictures, which land card by card. Every other key does nothing on the map: Tab, the arrows, Home, End, the function keys. The map is read, not steered.

Two ⌘ keys are the window's, on the map and in the strip alike, taken before the filter sees them (OverviewWindow.performKeyEquivalent, hand-written here): ⌘W closes, ⌘Q quits the app under the pointer and the overview stays up. Every other ⌘ key does nothing on the map.

5. One way out

Hand-written, from OverviewStore.send and OverlayWindowManager: a focus action (focus window, focus workspace) makes the store say onShotDone(restoreFocus: false), and a strip that is over (following returned nil) makes it say onShotDone(restoreFocus: true). The host hides the window either way, bringing the focused app forward when the focus did not just move there. Escape, ⌘W and a click on the backdrop hide it directly.
